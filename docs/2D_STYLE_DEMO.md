# 2D style demonstration: compiled prompts

Made by `src/domain/demo2d.ts`. These are the requests Production One would send, for one character (Mira), one prop (Brass lantern) and one place (Harbour office), in five representative 2D styles. **They are prompts only: nothing was generated and no image model was called.** They show that each style reaches the request; they do not show how any model draws it.

## Clean digital cel animation

Project file: `samples/2d-style-demo/2d-clean-digital-cel.pone`

### Mira: Turnaround, Front

```text
Production reference image: Front of the character Mira (Turnaround sheet).
Full body standing FRONT view, dead-on to the camera: shoulders and hips square and level, weight even on both feet, no twist and no hip tilt, arms slightly away from the body, feet apart, head to feet fully inside the frame.
Confirmed character identity: age 30s, female, harbour pilot. Must never drift: never without the scar.
Description (the person's own words): A weathered harbour pilot with a short grey-streaked braid, a long navy coat with brass buttons, a patched satchel and a small scar through her left eyebrow.
Visual style bible: 2D art style "Clean digital cel animation" (Drawn and cel animation): Crisp digital cel look: confident uniform outlines, flat fills and one clear shadow shape. Line: Clean, smooth vector-like outline of even weight, closed shapes, no sketchiness. Shape language: Simple, clear, geometric-organic shapes that animate cleanly. Proportions: Consistent, designed proportions; model-sheet precise. Palette: Flat local colours with a small, harmonised palette per character. Shading: Flat fills with one hard-edged shadow shape and an optional highlight shape; no gradients. Texture: None: smooth flat colour. Edges: Crisp and clean everywhere. Detail level: Low to moderate; patterns simplified. Perspective: Drawn perspective with clear, simple vanishing points; characters sit in the picture plane like animation layouts. Background treatment: Painted or inked background kept simpler and softer than the characters so they read first. Animation model-sheet clarity: the subject alone on the plain flat field, even flat lighting, no cast shadow, no scenery, construction and proportions easy to read. This is one flat 2D picture drawn in this style. It is not a 3D render and not a photograph. Avoid: soft airbrushed gradients; photographic or 3D shading; painterly texture; sketchy overlapping lines..
The background is one flat, uniform neutral mid-grey field (about 18% grey) from corner to corner: no gradient, no vignette, no floor, no wall, no horizon, and no cast or contact shadow, glow or colour spill anywhere on it. Even, shadowless lighting on the subject, no text, no labels, no borders, no watermark.
Keep the same face, body proportions, hairstyle and costume construction in every view of this character.
Exactly ONE single image of ONE subject in ONE view. Do not make a grid, contact sheet, collage, split-screen, triptych or several views side by side; no repeated copies of the subject.
The entire figure from the top of the head to the soles of the feet must be fully visible with clear margin; nothing cropped.
Keep the chosen 2D style consistently across the whole image; do not add photographic skin pores, film grain, lens bloom, depth of field or 3D material shading. Keep fills clean and even, without unintended noise or banding.
```
