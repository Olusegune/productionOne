# Production One 0.60.0

- **Find missing pictures shows a preview first.** After you choose the folder, "Review matches before relinking" lists every missing picture, the file found for it (or that the folder has none) and what you had decided about it. Tick the ones to link and press Relink N files. Nothing changes until you do; nothing is copied, moved, generated or deleted.
- **Review: compare, zoom and keep moving.** Full image or Top crop, zoom in and out (Ctrl and the mouse wheel too), and Sync zoom keeps both pictures in step. A Review summary strip shows the reference, your decision and the technical checks, and a bar at the bottom shows how many are left, what is Next, and previous and next arrows.
