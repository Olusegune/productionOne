# Production One 0.29.0

A design refresh on top of the 0.28.x work. One typeface (Inter, tuned for Windows and for large and small sizes), neutral charcoal surfaces and thin separators in the spirit of Apple's and DaVinci Resolve's interfaces, thumbnails that take the shape of their sheet, and a light theme that is readable on every screen. Updated user guide and screenshots.

Also in this release: everything from 0.28.3 to 0.28.9 (no lost work on New or Open, Add from image, per-sheet Shape and Backdrop, one-image sheet export, casting lines, personality and Never fields, a fix for a Wardrobe costume front that showed a different person, and the fixes found by a full systems and stress run).

Installers are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run.
