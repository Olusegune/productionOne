# Production One 0.69.0

- **Lighting test for skin.** Under Skin & Surface, make the same approved person and skin profile under four lights: neutral diffuse, soft side, directional and backlight (the face stays readable). The lighting is stored apart from the skin profile and tied to the exact skin version it was made with; approve a newer skin profile and the test is marked out of date, nothing else. Each picture is a new candidate from an image model, never the same picture relit and never a colour filter; warm light and natural undertones are not "corrected". For each light the screen says what that light is expected to do to colour and asks identity, complexion and surface as separate questions; keeping a light as a reference needs identity and complexion confirmed. Generating is explicit and the only thing that spends money.
- **Skin surface check.** "Check skin surface (advisory)" is free and runs on this computer. It measures the face area of the approved original and a candidate (fine-detail energy, share of very bright pixels, average colour) and can flag possible excessive smoothing, exaggerated texture, uniform shine or a complexion shift, each with the real numbers as evidence, how uncertain it is, what to do and the checker version. Findings appear in Review as open items (category L, Skin surface) and are never resolved automatically. The states are Not checked, Needs review, Passed for the checks performed and Unable to check. It lists what it cannot see (identity, aging, marks, makeup, detail across views, sharpening halos) as needing your review.
- **Where human-skin rules do not apply.** A stylized style, a character declared as a machine, and a transformation stage that replaces human skin (scales, metal, decay, another material) get no human-skin wording and no human-skin checks, and the screen says why. The sheet studio's skin wording is now per look.
- **Ages.** An age variant of a photorealistic character carries the approved skin profile plus age-appropriate fine detail for that age. Aging is fine surface detail only: never dirt, damage or heavy weathering, and the same marks and complexion are kept.
- **Documentation.** docs/SKIN_SURFACE_GUIDE.md (how to use Skin & Surface, provider notes, known limits), docs/SKIN_SURFACE_PLAN.md (plan and what was found), and docs/UI_REDESIGN_PLAN.md (the reading of the supplied UI redesign and the route and action mapping; no redesign code yet).
- Projects keep working: everything new is optional; older projects open and save as before; a fuzz test still damages saved projects (now including lighting tests) without crashing any screen.
- Known: no real refinement or lighting run has been judged yet, so how the skin looks on real characters is untested and needs your eyes; the checks are heuristics with no measured accuracy and work best on the head-and-shoulders picture; masks are not supported by any provider adapter, so regional wishes are wording only; a project opened from a folder other than the app's own cannot be used as a reference until its pictures are inside the app's folder (the safety rule from 0.67.0). Mac files are not included (the signing certificate secret is invalid).
