# Production One 0.68.0

- **Skin & Surface (photorealistic characters).** A new section in Age & appearance for the look you want for a character's skin: natural texture, restrained highlights, believable tone. Presets (Natural, Clean portrait, Weathered, Custom) fill structured choices; you set texture detail, surface finish, tonal variation, fine facial hair and retouching, list distinctive marks with where they are, say what must be protected, and can open regional controls (forehead, cheeks, nose, eye area, lips, neck, hands). Makeup, complexion and undertone are your own words and nothing else is chosen from them; temporary conditions (sweat, dirt) are kept apart and never change the approved profile. It is only written into pictures for an approved profile on a photorealistic style, so 2D and stylized projects are never pushed toward realism. Setting it costs nothing.
- **Protected skin refinement.** "Generate refinement" (the only button that spends money; a card first says what will be made, kept and left alone, and the cost) makes two candidates from the character's approved picture: a head-and-shoulders and a full-front view, each from the approved original of that view as its one reference. The original is never changed; the candidates are filed under their own look name. Compare side by side with synchronized zoom, a before/after toggle and close-ups of forehead, eye area, cheeks and mouth. Approving needs your separate confirmation that identity is preserved: better-looking skin alone never passes. Request changes and Turn down keep everything in history. Approved refinements become a look for new sheets. Models edit the whole picture and cannot be limited to a region, and the app says so; a model without reference support is refused with what to do. A request refused on this computer is explained in plain words and is not counted as spend.
- **Vehicles.** A prop can be a vehicle: seats, size, entrances and features with the views each must appear in, power, intentional and never lists, with templates (car, truck, motorbike, aircraft, spacecraft, boat). The declared design goes into pictures; a person who would not sit upright inside, or more riders than seats, is flagged.
- **Preflight on the Wardrobe studio.** The "before you press the button" card now also covers garment previews and fitting an outfit.
- Projects keep working: everything new is optional; older projects open and save as before, and a fuzz test damages saved projects (now including skin profiles and vehicles) without crashing any screen.
- Known: no real-provider refinement has been judged yet, so how the skin looks on real characters is untested; masks are not supported by any provider adapter, so regional wishes are wording only; lighting-test sheets, skin quality checks and the age and transformation integration are not built yet; the vehicle size is not written into prompts automatically. A project opened from a folder other than the app's own cannot be refined until its pictures are inside the app's folder (the safety rule from 0.67.0). Mac files are not included (the signing certificate secret is invalid).
