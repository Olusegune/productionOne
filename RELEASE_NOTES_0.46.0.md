# Production One 0.46.0

**Change your mind, the newest models, sets and props you can add, and more video models.**

## New
- **Undo for decisions.** Approve a picture you rejected by mistake, reject an approved one, or press Undo approval / Undo reject / Not decided to put it back among those waiting. Nothing is deleted and nothing is generated. Available on view cards, in Review (new Approved list), in the full-size viewer (U) and on storyboard frames.
- **Newest image models in a drop-down:** Seedream 5.5, GPT Image 2.6, MiniMax H3, Nano Banana Pro and more, with every other image model listed below them. Nano Banana Pro works with a Kie.ai key alone.
- **Add to the bible:** add a character, costume, prop or set, with or without an image you already have. The bible always shows all four groups.
- **More video models:** Seedance 2.5, Kling 4.0, Wan 3.0, HappyHorse 1.1, MiniMax H3, PixVerse V6 and Grok Imagine 1.5 (marked not verified until run), in Deliver and in the motion demo.

## Changed
- The motion demo's video models are grouped by price class (lower cost first, premium and other models below); it starts on a lower-cost model.

## Fixed
- Set pictures fill the frame (no grey bars); voice-only characters such as a PA VOICE are not drawn in storyboard shots; Seedance 2.5 now runs from a picture.
- Scripts with headings like `SCENE 2 — INT. OFFICE — NIGHT` are now read as screenplays: speakers and lines are found, and beats in one room no longer become separate sets.
- The picture-based motion demo no longer lists text-to-video models; a hiccup on Kie's documentation site is no longer reported as a missing model.
- The Dark / Light switch is no longer squashed; the sheet controls no longer scroll in a box of their own; the Shape setting is no longer squeezed to nothing.
