# Production One 0.14.3

## Asset Lab and Golden Path

Production One now provides a unified Asset Lab for identity, motion, interaction, sound, and continuity. Motion studies adapt to the asset type: character acting and expression, costume drape and wind, prop function and damage, and set/environment camera, lighting, weather, and scale studies.

Asset direction is editable and preserved with the project, including motion, interaction, sound, prompt, and character identity fields.
