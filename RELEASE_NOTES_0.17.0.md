# Production One 0.17.0

Voice engines and performance direction.

- Choose a voice engine in the Voice audition: Windows stand-in, Chatterbox, Qwen TTS or any speech server with a /v1/audio/speech route on this computer or your network.
- Direct each line: physical state, emotion, laugh/chuckle/cough/grunt/sigh/gasp cues. Anything the chosen engine cannot do is listed as "Not applied" with what to do instead. Talking while eating or carrying a load is NOT something speech models do reliably; it is saved in the casting brief.
- Limits: the HTTP engines were not tested against a live server. Inline sound tags depend on the Chatterbox version you run. Suno via Kie, VoiceKiller and Wazobia are not included. Installers are unsigned.
