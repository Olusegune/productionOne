# The world of THE RENEWAL: the Bureau of Infernal Affairs

A small, consistent world for testing Production One with its own pictures. The three test characters come from the owner's own artwork, which is **not** kept in this repository.

## The setting: Port Meridian
A rainy harbour city of about two million people, near enough to our own that nothing needs explaining: ferries, studios, glass towers, a municipal annex on every second street. The Bureau of Infernal Affairs does not hide; it is simply boring. Its offices look like every other licensing department, and most people never think about what it licenses.

## The rules (so the story stays believable)
1. **The Bureau files; it never compels.** Every arrangement it processes was signed freely, in writing. It cannot lie on a form.
2. **Instruments of Fortune** are leather folios, renewed every seven years. Success is real while an Instrument is in force.
3. **Clause nine, the Sunset clause.** At each renewal the signatory may decline by signing a *Notice of Satisfaction*. The Bureau must offer it, but almost nobody reads that far, because the clerk who offers it is bound to say it aloud only once.
4. **Declining has a cost, and an honest one:** the borrowed shine goes (the gold records dull), but what the person built before the Instrument stays.
5. Paper is the magic: stamps, queue numbers and folders. A cracked stamp is bad luck; the display stuck on 7 is the same number as the years of an Instrument.

## Look
Wet city, teal and ink-blue night, neon red as the accent colour (red eyes, bow tie, LED glasses, the booth lights), warm gold only where fortune is present. Stylised 3D animation, slightly caricatured proportions: very long limbs, big expressive faces.

## Characters (as drawn)
- **VENOMSPUR**: a very tall, very thin Bureau clerk; bald, pointed ears, red eyes behind round black glasses; black tailcoat, black waistcoat, red bow tie and pocket square; hands clasped; black polished shoes. Precise, patient, dry; secretly tired of the system.
- **KID LANTERN** (Tobi Adewale, 19): a thin young rapper; short hair, a thin chin beard; black bomber jacket with red trim, black T-shirt, gold chain with a round medallion, dark flared jeans, white sneakers; holds a microphone in his right hand, left hand in his pocket. Watchful, proud, in a hurry.
- **AURUM** (Deon Asher, 34): a hip hop megastar; braided hair, beard, tattoos on neck, chest and hands; oversized red puffer jacket, heavy gold chains, red LED glasses, black cargo trousers, sneakers with glowing orange soles; a microphone as a habit. Generous and loud in public, worn out in private.

## Scenes
1. Annex C, morning: Venomspur files the day's forms; Aurum's renewal arrives.
2. Booth A, night: Kid Lantern is stuck on a line; a Notice of Offer slides under the door.
3. The waterfront at dawn: Venomspur meets Aurum at the end of the pier.
4. Aurum's penthouse: the renewal, and clause nine.
5. Booth A, later: Venomspur makes Kid Lantern the same offer and asks the question nobody asks.
6. The penthouse, night: Aurum signs the Notice of Satisfaction.
7. Annex C: the queue display moves to 8; the stamp cracks; Kid Lantern tears up his Notice of Offer.
8. The waterfront, dawn: Kid Lantern writes his own; Aurum listens.

## Uses in testing
Voices (three clear registers: dry old bureaucrat, young rapper, warm loud star), music (a theme per character and a score for the pier), revisions (a second take of a front view to compare), wardrobe variants (Venomspur's rain-soaked coat; Aurum in plain clothes).
