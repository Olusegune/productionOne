# Production One 0.67.0

- **Production bible, grouped.** The Design list has a switcher: Characters, Assets (costumes and props), Worlds (sets and environments) and an Overview page that shows what you have, species and factions, relationships, frozen scenes and anything out of date. Nothing is restructured: the six workspaces are as before.
- **Relationships.** Record who owns what, who sits or holds what, how big one thing is next to another. They are approved by you, pinned to the version they were agreed for (flagged "Needs a look" if it changes), checked in the blocking map and in Review (a rapper in Venomspur's chair, or standing through the desk), and kept in the package.
- **Props.** A prop's size and how it is held, checked against a character's hand, arm and height, with a plain sentence for prompts. Guidance, not measurement.
- **Anatomy, machine parts and powers.** Say what a being must have (limbs, digits, eyes, intentional features, never-list), what a robot, android, mech or suit must show in each view, and a power's one visual language across six states. What you declare is written into new pictures; a deliberate six-fingered hand is never flagged as a mistake. A picture is only called transparent if its file really is. Hero and villain are story roles, not bodies.
- **Species and factions.** Members inherit; differences are proposals you accept or keep as exceptions. Nothing you approved is ever overwritten.
- **Scene continuity.** Freeze a scene to the exact version of each look; frozen scenes never change when looks are revised later. Branch a scene ("night version").
- **Templates and preflight.** One-click starting points for beings, machines and powers (they only fill the form). Age and Transformations show what will be made, kept, left alone and affected, and the cost, before you press the button.
- **Vision QA for candidates.** "Check against what it was made from" on outfits, conditions, ages and stages: record facts and simple questions to a local vision model (which side, same person, worn or not), each asked twice, honestly labelled; never a pass it did not earn. Pictures are shrunk before they go to a local model (a full-size picture could not be answered at all before).
- **Colour and icons.** Every area of the left navigation, the bible list, the asset tabs, statuses and the new cards have their own icon colour (checked for contrast in light and dark) so a screen can be scanned.
- **Security.** A project from someone else can no longer make the app read any picture on the computer for a provider or a local model, or put any file in a package: only files in the app's assets folder are read.
- **Fixes from a full QA pass.** Keyboard users can now move between tabs (arrow keys, Home, End); the Script asset card is no longer cut off; the bible list stays a list in narrow windows; long names no longer push buttons out; the icon rail is easier to hit; studio headers no longer have a line through the text.
- Projects keep working: everything new is an optional addition; older projects open and save as before, and damaged records are dropped without crashing (a fuzz test damages a saved project 1500 ways).
- Known: nothing yet reads pictures for the new records (limb counts, parts seen, transparency are what you enter); prop sizes are not added to prompts automatically; no vehicle or environment records; the preflight card is on Age and Transformations only. Mac files are not included (the signing certificate secret is invalid).
