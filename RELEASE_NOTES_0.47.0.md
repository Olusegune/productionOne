# Production One 0.47.0

**Choose your format first (TikTok, Instagram, cinema), a clearer storyboard card, and a fuller guide.**

## New
- **Format** on the Projects screen and in the Director's room: Widescreen 16:9, Cinema 21:9, TikTok / Reels / Shorts 9:16, Instagram portrait 4:5, Square 1:1, and more shapes. Saved with the project.
- Storyboard frames, the card shapes, the previews and the video shape on Deliver all follow it.
- Change it any time: frames already made keep their shape and are tagged "Made at 16:9". Nothing is regenerated or paid for until you press Regenerate.

## Changed
- The storyboard card is easier to read: a status badge, one-line name, two lines of action, small tags for size, lens, movement and time, and one main button. The rest is in the menu.
- The guide has a section on choosing a format, a rewritten Direct section, and new screenshots.
