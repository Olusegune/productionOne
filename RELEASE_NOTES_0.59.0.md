# Production One 0.59.0

- **Shape & proportions workspace.** Set proportions (Identity card, with a new icon) opens a full-window workspace: Body, Face and Advanced controls with typed numbers and per-control reset; a large figure with a head-unit ruler, measurement brackets and a Compare view against Natural; a Proportion check; the approved picture for reference; Apply to (this character, or also the project world profile); your own saved presets; and Linked proportions. Edits are a draft until you press Apply proportions; Generate design preview is a separate step that spends one image request. The height control is now called Total height (head units).
- **Find missing pictures.** When pictures are not on this computer, Deliver, the failure list and the command palette offer "Find the files…". You choose a folder (folders inside it are searched too); pictures are matched by the name they were saved under and linked back, with your approve or reject decision restored when the project remembers it. Nothing is copied, generated or deleted.
