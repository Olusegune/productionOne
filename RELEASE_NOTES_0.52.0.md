# Production One 0.52.0

Optional design tools. Nothing changes in a project until you use it; older projects open and render exactly as before.

- **Shape and proportions:** target proportions per character with a schematic preview, starter profiles, stylization, overrides, undo and warnings.
- **Camera and lighting:** camera direction with a field-of-view readout, camera and lighting presets, coverage suggestions, lighting tests, and a top-down blocking map.
- **Character layers and fit:** proportions carried into every sheet and frame, set and prop fit proposals you approve, Scale and Fit sheet, redesign of imported pictures.
- **Visual review:** checks against your records, an optional vision check on this computer, and bounded corrections. Nothing is approved for you.
- **Looks:** non-destructive grading with named looks and a colour script. No LUTs yet.
- **Packages:** `design_records.json` added; packages containing keys, credentials or local model files are refused.
- Also since 0.51.2: the Assistant can write with Ollama, LM Studio, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter.

Not checked: nothing was tried against a live image or vision model; installers were not run on a clean machine. Mac builds are unsigned.
