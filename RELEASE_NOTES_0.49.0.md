# Production One 0.49.0

**Real upscalers: Real-ESRGAN (free), Topaz and Magnific (paid).**

## New
- **Image quality > Enlarge > Enlarge with**: Smooth (free), Real-ESRGAN (free, through ComfyUI), Topaz (through your Kie key), Magnific (its own key).
- Paid methods work on one picture, run only when you press their button, warn about cost first, and refuse an oversized result before anything is sent.
- Results and errors show inside the Image quality panel.

## Be aware
- **Topaz and Magnific are untested.** Kie's Topaz service answered with a timeout error on every real try, so I could not confirm it works. Magnific was built from its documentation and no key was available. Real-ESRGAN needs ComfyUI and `RealESRGAN_x4plus.pth`.
- If you try Topaz and see charges for a failed task, check your Kie dashboard.

## Not checked
- A successful Topaz, Magnific or Real-ESRGAN result in the real app, and the light theme.
